What I Can Do

A quick tour of how to use your AI Chief of Staff

I’m your private AI Chief of Staff — here to handle the busywork, remember what matters, and run on a schedule so things don’t slip. Tap any section below to see what’s possible.

💬 Just talk to me

The simplest way to start is to type whatever’s on your mind into the chat box. I can:

  • Answer questions, draft emails, brainstorm, or work through a decision
  • Summarize long documents you paste or drop into the chat
  • Plan a project, break down a goal, or write a first draft of anything
  • Search the web for current information

Each chat is its own thread — click New Chat in the sidebar to start fresh.

🧠 Personalize me — your Knowledge Base

Click Personalize in the sidebar to teach me about you. The more you fill in, the more I sound like a teammate who already knows your world.

  • Your Details — your name, role, what you’d like me to call myself
  • How I Act — tone, formatting, how forward I should be
  • About You — what matters to you, who’s in your world
  • Knowledge Base — paste your brand book, style guide, business overview, and operating rules. You can also drag and drop .txt, .md, .docx, or .pdf files in.
  • System Prompt — advanced: the master instructions I run on

Hit Save, then Reboot to apply changes.

⏰ Scheduled Tasks — things I do on a schedule

Scheduled Tasks are prompts I run automatically — a morning briefing at 6 AM, a Friday review at 4:30 PM, hydration nudges through the workday. Here’s how to make one:

  1. Click Scheduled Tasks in the sidebar.
  2. Click Recipes at the top right to browse 8 ready-made templates. Pick one close to what you want and copy the prompt.
  3. Click Open Task Editor, paste the prompt, and edit the wording to fit you.
  4. Set when it should run (daily / weekly / monthly) and how you want to receive it (desktop, Telegram, email).
  5. Save. I’ll run it on the schedule and deliver the result through your chosen channel.

The Scheduled Tasks list is split into Daily, Weekly, and Monthly tabs so you can see at a glance what’s on each cadence.

✍️ Content Writer — one-click SEO blog + social posts

Click Content Writer in the sidebar to launch a full content pipeline in a single chat. One button, end-to-end:

  1. I research keyword ideas in your niche using DataForSEO (search volume + difficulty).
  2. I read the top 3 SERP results and pick an angle that brings your brand’s perspective.
  3. I fact-check every citable claim live before using it.
  4. I generate a hero image (1792×1024) sized for blog headers.
  5. I draft an 800–1200 word article in your brand voice (from your Knowledge Base).
  6. I post the full article + image inline so you can review and request changes.
  7. When you click ✓ Approve & Publish, I save blog-post.md to ~/Desktop/Blogs/YYYY-MM-DD-slug/.
  8. Then click ⚡ Create social content and I’ll spin out 5 platform-tailored posts (Google Business Profile, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Medium) directly in the chat for copy-paste, plus an Instagram-square version of the hero. All 5 are also saved as .md files alongside the blog as a backup.

First-time setup: the Content Writer panel asks for three things — an OpenAI key (for image generation), DataForSEO login (for keyword research), and your brand book (so I write in your voice, not mine). Once all three are ✓, the ▶ Write My Next Blog Post button unlocks.

Nothing ever publishes externally — output goes to your Desktop only. You copy-paste into your blog editor and social platforms when you’re ready.

📁 Documents & files

Drag any file straight into the chat input — PDFs, Word docs, text, markdown, images. I’ll read it and we can talk about it.

For documents you want me to always know about (your brand book, a key contract, your operating manual), put them in Personalize → Knowledge Base → Documents & References instead. Then I’ll have that context in every conversation.

🌐 Browser & web tasks

I can browse the web for you — read pages, fill in forms, summarize what I find. Just ask in plain English, e.g. “check the top three competitors’ pricing pages and summarize their tiers.”

📱 Connect Telegram (mobile)

You can chat with me from your phone via Telegram — same memory, same context, anywhere you are. Open Personalize → Connectors (or your settings panel) and follow the Telegram setup steps.

🔒 Privacy

Your data lives on this Mac/PC only. Conversations go to Anthropic’s API (that’s how I think) — nothing else leaves your machine. Your API key is stored in the OS keychain.
